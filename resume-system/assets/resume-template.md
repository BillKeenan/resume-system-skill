# Resume Template & Formatting Rules — [Name]

## Layout (two pages max)
1. Name, contact line, LinkedIn
2. Summary (3–4 lines)
3. Core competencies (optional, 6–10 terms; ATS-heavy targets only)
4. Experience (reverse chronological)
5. Earlier experience (one line each)
6. Education / patents / certifications

## Role depth table (edit to match your history)
| Role | Bullets |
|------|---------|
| Current role | 4–6 |
| Previous role | 4–6 |
| Role 3 | 3–4 |
| Role 4 | 3–4 |
| Role 5 | 2–3 |
| Founder / early roles | 1–2, or consolidate into one line |
| Early-career | Omit |

## Page-fit cut order
1. Consolidate early/founder roles
2. Trim middle-role bullets by one
3. Remove core competencies

## Bullet formula
`[Action verb] + [what] + [resulting in] + [quantified outcome]`

## Style
- Month + year dates, consistent format
- Past tense for past roles, present for current
- No first-person pronouns
- Company-line under each title: one phrase on what the company is and its scale
